# Questionnaires and response forms

**Status:** DRAFT (2026-11-21). Sessions are run in Korean. English source items are listed here; the Korean wording used in the sessions will be added next to each item, with the translation source, before the pilot.

## A. Background (before the session)
1. Participant type: staff / external
2. Age band: 20–29 / 30–39 / 40–49 / 50+
3. Current role (free text; published only as a broad category)
4. Experience in building operation or building services: none / under 1 year / 1–5 years / over 5 years
5. Have you used a building automation or energy management screen? yes / no
6. How often do you use 3D software or 3D viewers? never / a few times a year / monthly / weekly
7. How often do you use AI chat tools? rarely / sometimes / daily

## B. Per trial (12 trials)
1. Decision: accept the answer / reject the answer
2. Confidence in that decision: 1 (not at all) – 7 (completely)
3. Click the equipment or space you think is responsible (logged as object ID)

## C. Trust in the system (after each condition; 7-point agreement)
Items from Jian, Bisantz and Drury (2000), Int. J. Cognitive Ergonomics 4(1).
1. The system is deceptive.
2. The system behaves in an underhanded manner.
3. I am suspicious of the system's intent, action, or outputs.
4. I am wary of the system.
5. The system's actions will have a harmful or injurious outcome.
6. I am confident in the system.
7. The system provides security.
8. The system has integrity.
9. The system is dependable.
10. The system is reliable.
11. I can trust the system.
12. I am familiar with the system.

Items 1–5 are reverse-scored. Item wording must be checked against the original paper before use.

## D. Workload (after each condition; NASA Raw-TLX, 0–100 in steps of 5)
Mental demand, physical demand, temporal demand, performance, effort, frustration.

## E. Usability (once, at the end; UMUX-Lite, 7-point agreement)
1. This system's capabilities meet my requirements.
2. This system is easy to use.

## F. Closing interview (about 4 minutes)
1. What made you decide that an answer was wrong?
2. Was there a moment when the 3D evidence got in your way?
3. What would you need before using this in real work?

## G. Consent form (blank template)
To be added. It must state: purpose; voluntary participation and the right to stop at any time; what is recorded (screen and audio only); that data are stored under a participant code; **that de-identified data will be published**; compensation; and a contact person.
